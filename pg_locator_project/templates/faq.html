Booking & Move-in

On any property details page, click the "Contact Owner" button. You will be provided with the owner's phone number and email to directly schedule a convenient time for a physical visit.

Typically, you will need a government-issued photo ID (Aadhar Card, Passport), a passport-sized photograph, and a letter from your employer or college ID. Specific owners may request additional documentation.

Payments & Refunds

Security deposits are handled directly between you and the property owner. The standard deposit is usually 1 to 2 months' rent. Ensure you get a written receipt or rental agreement mentioning the deposit amount.

For Property Owners

Sign up as an Owner, log in to your dashboard, and click "Add Property". Fill in the required details, upload clear photos, and submit. Our team will review the listing before it goes live.

Standard approval takes 24-48 hours. If you opt for the "Verified" badge, our field agents will contact you to schedule a physical inspection within 3 business days.

Trust & Security

We verify the contact information of all registered owners. Furthermore, seekers are encouraged to leave honest reviews. Properties with consistent negative safety reviews are investigated and potentially delisted.

While rental agreements are strictly between the seeker and owner, our Customer Support team can mediate severe disputes. We recommend documenting all agreements and payments in writing.
